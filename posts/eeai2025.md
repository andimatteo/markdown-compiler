Hi, this is Andrea.

This October I attended EEAI2025 (European Conference on EDGE AI Technologies and Applications) in Naples.
I took this as an opportunity to visit my granparents and relatives (my parents are from Naples),
~(technically from Caserta, and someone from Caserta would hate being referred to as someone from Naples. You know, grotesque tribalism is really a thing for Italians)~,
I hadn't seem them in a while, I always pretend to be too busy with
uni, work and research.

> but recently my grandpa passed away, so I started giving things
> the right time *for me*, and the right priorities *for me*.

I also took this as an opportunity to visit Naples
mostly by myself and sometimes with my cousin, my personal [Cicero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cicero),
that studies at [Federico II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II).
The organization committee really bet on the right horse,
this is a photo from the balcony of the conference, and there's only one
thing I can say: ["mamma mia, quanto e' bella Napoli"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HLfAHmLzdLI)

![naples](naples.jpg)

It was a beautiful place and the weather was ~mostly~ beaufiful as well.
This trip was also full of coincidences. Just to give
you an idea: my dad's name is *Giovan Giuseppe*,
and the room I presented in is called *room San Giovan Giuseppe*, named after
the Saint. San Giovan Giuseppe spent there last years of his life. What a coincidence, really.
I never expected to find my father's name anywhere beyond the confines of his own ID,
everyone just calls him *Peppe* or, if you are a really
close friend of his, *Pino* 
~seriously, don't do it if he doesn't know you well, he's gonna look very strange at you~.

> if you don't trust me how rare his name is, just
> check it yourself on [italian national statistics center](https://www.istat.it/dati/calcolatori/contanomi/)

It has been my first time presenting alone my own paper at a conference.
If I told you I completely enjoyed the conference I would be lying,
maybe I haven't attended the right sessions, maybe I haven't met 
the right people. I created a few connections but expected way more
from an international conference. Btw, this is a photo of me looking
*very serious*, presenting my *very serious* work in my *very serious* suit
~please, catch the irony :)~.
