Hi, this is Andrea.

This April I presented at WCNC (Wireless Communications and Networking
Conference) in Kuala Lumpur. It has been my first time in Asia and my first
experience as a session chair ~which means means studying 4-5 papers
and preparing questions in advance~. Kuala Lumpur was really hot and humid.
I spent most of my spare time walking and ~sweating~ visiting
around. I definetely wasn't expecting so many things, such as the
asian version of Uber but with red-lights tracking: Grab; the amazing
KLCC (Kuala Lumpur City Center) skyscapers,

> No like, seriously, I was in San Francisco 2 weeks before and I can
> tell you that SF looks really, really old compared.

the odd stink of [durians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durian) all around the city,

> I discovered that people from China do come in KL to get a special 
> type of durian, that grows only in [Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musang_King)

the fact that it is a left driving country
~then I remembered that it was an English colony~, and the almost
obsessive order Asians follow when boarding trains/metros: there
are queues to the right and left of the doors, and the middle is
kept clear so people can get off easily (we must import this in Europe).

![order on trains](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6509cf14-1354-4091-8b23-12c08fc51c57)

The paper I presented is
[Incremental Vision Transformers for Zero-Day Attack Detection in NextG Wireless Networks](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?tp=&arnumber=11555756),
with Emilio Paolini, Prof. Valcarenghi and Prof. Andriolli.
And I chaired a UAV session mainly based on reinforcement learning techniques
for drone swarms task scheduling.
