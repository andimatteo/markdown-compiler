Hi, this is Andrea.

This summer I'm taking Computer Graphics @ Stanford and I thought:
"why not implementing a rotating torus in the terminal in C?".

I know, this might sound a bit [amarcod](https://www.treccani.it/vocabolario/amarcord/),
but, after writing a ray-tracer in Python that actually runs in Blender,
doing some very simple things from scratch with no
dependecies feels relaxing. I felt I had so many concepts in mind
and just wanted to express some of them.

So there it is, a speedrun completed in `45:27,06` with no WEB access
and no GenAI tools (I know, this is not impressive at all, and I'm not
trying to impress anyone. But be advised, only the parametric
equations of a torus won't be enough :) ).

During the course might come back to add or fix some stuff ~or just forget about this project forever~.
